Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Migo Games Studio handles information for our mobile games, including Crossword Master: Clue Game.

Last updated: May 6, 2026

1. Who We Are

Migo Games Studio is an independent mobile game studio that creates puzzle and word games. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile game Crossword Master: Clue Game and our website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account to play Crossword Master: Clue Game. We do not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information inside the game.

However, the game may use third-party services that can automatically collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, crash information, and usage data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game may use third-party services to support advertising, analytics, app distribution, and app performance. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Console tools and related Google services

You can learn more about how Google handles data by visiting Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising

Crossword Master: Clue Game may display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and advertising data to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve ad quality.

Depending on your device settings and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can manage ad personalization through your device settings or Google account settings.

5. App Permissions

The game may use internet access to load ads, connect with Google services, and support app functionality. We do not use app permissions to collect personal contact information.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our game is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it if necessary.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information related to our apps and website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We do not directly store personal user accounts or personal profiles in our game. Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies and legal requirements.

9. Your Choices

You can control certain data and advertising settings from your device or Google account. You may also uninstall the game at any time to stop further data collection by the app.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our apps, you can contact us at:
